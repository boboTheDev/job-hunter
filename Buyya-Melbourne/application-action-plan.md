## Supervisor email structure

Use a short email rather than pasting the full plan:

1. One sentence identifying degree, current institution, expected completion, and research/publication status.
2. Two sentences naming the proposed problem and how it extends the lab's work.
3. Two sentences on implementation/research evidence.
4. A direct request for a brief discussion and in-principle support, mentioning the 11 October deadline without pressuring him.
5. Attach CV, two-page note, transcripts/results, and publication list/papers.
