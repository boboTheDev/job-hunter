# Entry requirements and current scholarship competitiveness

Sources: [course entry-requirements tab](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/find/courses/graduate/doctor-of-philosophy-engineering-and-it/entry-requirements/), [international graduate-research entry requirements](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/graduate-research/international-applications/entry-requirements), and [current fees/scholarships tab](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/find/courses/graduate/doctor-of-philosophy-engineering-and-it/fees/)  
Checked: 7 October 2026

## Summary and extracted facts

International applicants must have a qualification assessed as at least equivalent to an Australian honours degree, or comparable research experience. Formal equivalency is decided during assessment. Applicants must meet the course-specific English standard, obtain in-principle supervisor support for most graduate-research programs, and study full-time for student-visa purposes. Meeting minimum requirements does not guarantee selection.

The current FEIT scholarship page says successful research/scholarship applicants typically have strong nominated-supervisor support, rank in the top 5% of their graduating class, show research potential through a final-year Bachelor/Master research project, and hold a qualification from an institution with a recognized research profile.

The page describes a University of Melbourne equivalent score of 80% as usually meeting the research admission minimum but not usually being scholarship-competitive. It identifies 85% as an indicative score for local and international Melbourne Research Scholarship recipients, explicitly without a guarantee. Publications, professional experience, and institutional research profile may marginally affect the score.

## Applicant-specific critical points

- The applicant has a First Class BEng (Hons), a Master of Engineering scheduled for completion in 2026, two first-author IEEE conference papers, IELTS 8, cloud/DevOps experience, and relevant technical awards. These are credible research-readiness signals.
- Scholarship competitiveness cannot be inferred from degree labels. The actual transcripts, class rank/equivalency, Master research component, and thesis/report evidence are decisive.
- Verify the IELTS test date and exact course English rule; a score can be strong yet unusable if outside the accepted validity window.
- If the Master thesis is incomplete or ungraded, supply the strongest available research evidence and final results as soon as issued.
