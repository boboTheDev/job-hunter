# Graduate research and international application process

Sources: [graduate research overview](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/study-with-us/graduate-research), [international applications](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/graduate-research/international-applications/applications), and [international fees and payments](https://study.unimelb.edu.au/how-to-apply/graduate-research/international-applications/fees-and-payments)  
Checked: 7 October 2026

## Summary and extracted requirements

Applications are lodged through the University's online portal. At minimum, an applicant needs academic transcripts and a CV; course-specific documents can add further requirements. Most research applications require a proposal and evidence of supervisor support. The University says a proposal should state the problem, objectives or hypotheses, significance, and proposed method.

Applicants provide two referees. Where the last course was completed less than five years ago, they should be academic referees. Referees receive an online reference request after submission. Publications must be listed using the University's specified bibliographic formats; work in preparation may be included only when clearly labeled.

International research students must study full-time and obtain the appropriate visa after receiving an offer. The University reports that eligible graduate-research applicants are automatically considered for relevant Graduate Research Scholarships, though some other awards require separate applications. A fee offset may cover up to four years for a doctorate. Applicants without a tuition-fee offset may be offered a fee-paying place; the current international fees page states a minimum AUD 17,000 deposit when accepting without a tuition offset.

## Critical points

- Automatic scholarship consideration does not mean automatic funding; a full, on-time application and high ranking remain essential.
- Supervisor support and two responsive referees are operational dependencies that cannot be produced at the last minute.
- “More than 300” international graduate research scholarships and “1,200+” scholarships/bursaries are university-wide counts, not probabilities for this applicant or lab.
- The applicant should state clearly if enrolment depends on both tuition remission and a living stipend.
- The proposal is assessed for research readiness, disciplinary fit, and available supervision. It should be specific enough to evaluate but explicitly open to refinement with Prof. Buyya.
