# iQuantum initiative

Source: [CLOUDS Lab iQuantum](https://clouds.cis.unimelb.edu.au/iquantum/)  
Checked: 7 October 2026

## Summary

The initiative addresses quantum software engineering, heterogeneous quantum languages/hardware, application deployment, and modeling/simulation of quantum environments. The page lists work on quantum-computing taxonomy, iQuantum simulation, QFaaS serverless quantum computing, DRL placement, hybrid quantum-classical search, quantum cloud review, environmental-monitoring applications, and learning-centric simulation. It links open-source iQuantum and QFaaS software.

## Critical points

- This is an active and distinctive lab direction, but it is not the best immediate proposal anchor for the current applicant profile, which shows stronger evidence in classical cloud/DevOps, distributed software, AI, networking, and IoT.
- A quantum topic would add a steep domain-learning dependency and make a near-term two-page proposal less credible unless the applicant can demonstrate quantum foundations not present in the current CV.
- The initiative still provides a useful long-term extension: the proposed orchestration abstractions could later accommodate hybrid quantum-classical serverless tasks.

## Proposal implication

Do not make quantum computing a core aim. Mention it, at most, as an out-of-scope future workload class after the classical edge-cloud contribution is validated.
