I am deeply interested in developing deep-learning solutions for web-based applications, particularly focusing on leveraging cloud computation to optimise large language models (LLMs) like GPT-4 for use in personal assistance and gamified open-world simulations.
My research interests extend to creating a morphable, self-hosted LLM tailored with specific knowledge bases. This model aims to be seamlessly integrated into metaverse characters or virtual assistants. Collaborating with Professor Lunchakorn's metaverses, I plan to customise the training data for each character, ensuring precise and contextually relevant interactions. The advantages of self-hosted, customizable LLMs include independence from subscription-based models, enhanced security and ownership of conversations, and adaptability to diverse environments where the LLM is applied. For instance, integrating custom-hosted LLMs into telecom companies' metaverses can enable more accurate and unrestricted user conversations in real time from real clients.
To build a GPT-like LLM, I will start by outlining the frameworks and programming languages essential for the development process. Given my solid foundation in Python programming, I will delve into the underlying principles of deep learning and natural language processing (NLP) algorithms, such as recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and transformer models.
Proposed steps to Build the LLM:
1. Data Collection and Preprocessing: Gathering a diverse dataset of text from various sources to train the LLM. This involves cleaning the data, removing duplicates, and preprocessing the text to make it suitable for training.
2. Model Architecture: Implementing a transformer-based architecture, similar to GPT models. This will involve designing the neural network layers, attention mechanisms, and positional encoding.
3. Training the Model: Utilising machine-learning libraries like TensorFlow and PyTorch to train the LLM on the collected dataset. This involves fine-tuning the model's parameters and optimising its performance.
4. Evaluation and Optimization: Evaluating the model's performance using metrics like perplexity, BLEU score, and human evaluation. Continual optimization will be done to enhance the model's accuracy, fluency, and understanding of contextual conversations.
5. Customization and Integration: Tailoring the LLM with specific knowledge bases (for example: a character from a book or a customer service agent from a company) and integrating it into web-based applications or metaverse characters. This customization ensures that the LLM can generate relevant and contextually appropriate responses based on the specific application.
Additionally, I have hands-on experience in building deep learning neural networks for Plant health surveillance and a neural network to detect pulmonary tuberculosis. These projects have equipped me with practical skills in implementing machine learning algorithms, data preprocessing, and model evaluation.
By combining my expertise in Python programming, deep learning, and NLP with the outlined steps, I am confident in my ability to develop a sophisticated and customizable GPT-like LLM tailored for web-based applications and metaverse characters. This research will contribute to advancing the field of AI and NLP, providing innovative solutions for personalised virtual assistants and open-world simulations.
