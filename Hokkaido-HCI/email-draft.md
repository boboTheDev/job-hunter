Dear Professor Sakamoto,

My name is Minn Bo Bo, and I am writing to ask whether you would consider supervising my doctoral research in the Human-Computer Interaction Lab at Hokkaido University.

I am the primary developer of MANGOs, MANGOs School, and MANGOs Scene Studio—an integrated educational-metaverse ecosystem. I designed and implemented the platforms' frontend and backend systems, real-time communication, authentication and authorization, databases, cloud infrastructure, and browser-based 3D authoring and publishing workflows. My work has been conducted under the guidance of Professor Lunchakorn Wuttisittikulkij at Chulalongkorn University, Bangkok, Thailand.

My research publications include work on authentication and identification for interconnected metaverses and on tokenomics and ecosystem design. For my PhD, I would like to move beyond platform implementation and investigate the human-computer interaction challenges of educational XR.

My provisional research direction is **“Human-Centered Authoring and Collaborative Learning in Interoperable Educational Metaverses.”** I am particularly interested in designing and empirically evaluating interaction techniques that allow educators and learners to create, navigate, and collaborate in accessible, multilingual XR learning environments. I believe this direction aligns with your research on VR interaction, usability, user interfaces, and interaction design.

I understand that Hokkaido University is planning an EXEX Doctoral Fellowship application period for April 2027 entrants from November 2 to November 13, 2026, subject to confirmation of the new funding scheme. The application requires the approval and recommendation of a prospective supervisor.

Would you be willing to review my background and discuss whether my proposed research could be suitable for doctoral supervision in your laboratory? If you consider the fit appropriate, I would also be grateful to know whether you could support my EXEX Fellowship application. If an April 2027 start is not feasible, I would appreciate your advice regarding the MEXT University Recommendation route for October 2027.

I have attached my CV, publication list, and a preliminary research proposal for your consideration. I would be pleased to provide further information or attend an online meeting at your convenience.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration.
