## Recommended PhD direction

### Provisional title

**Human-Centered Authoring and Collaborative Learning in Interoperable Educational Metaverses**

### Possible research questions

1. How can non-expert educators create effective interactive XR learning environments?
2. Which interaction and navigation techniques reduce cognitive load in educational VR?
3. How do shared identity, embodiment, and persistent avatars affect trust and collaboration?
4. How should accessibility and multilingual content be designed for educational XR?
5. How can user behavior and learning outcomes be evaluated across connected virtual environments?

Authentication and tokenomics should be presented as enabling infrastructure rather than the central HCI contribution. The primary contribution should be new interaction techniques, research prototypes, and empirical user evaluation.

## Scholarship opportunities

### 1. EXEX Doctoral Fellowship — best immediate target

For April 2027 doctoral entrants, Hokkaido University currently plans an application period of **November 2–13, 2026**. New guidelines are scheduled for **October 28, 2026**. The call is conditional on Hokkaido University's proposed new funding scheme receiving JST approval.

The current EXEX scheme provides:

- JPY 2,160,000 per year as a research fellowship/living allowance.
- JPY 400,000 per year plus possible additional research expenses.
- Career-development and training support.

The forthcoming scheme is expected to offer support at a similar level, but its final terms and amounts are not yet confirmed.

Applicants must obtain approval from their expected Hokkaido University supervisor, and the expected supervisor must submit a recommendation. The fellowship application and doctoral admission are separate processes.

Links:

- [EXEX Doctoral Fellowship application and schedule](https://sites.google.com/eis.hokudai.ac.jp/exexphd-fellow/application)
- [EXEX application requirements and materials](https://sites.google.com/eis.hokudai.ac.jp/exexphd-fellow/application/start-your-application)

**Recommended action:** Contact Professor Sakamoto immediately and ask whether he would consider supervision and support an EXEX application.

### 2. MEXT University Recommendation — strong alternative

The Graduate School of Information Science and Technology confirms that:

- The MEXT University Recommendation route is available only for October enrollment.
- Guidelines are expected around winter.
- The number of recommendations is extremely limited.
- An applicant must first contact a prospective supervisor and obtain preliminary approval.

Hokkaido University's currently listed MEXT allowance for doctoral students is JPY 145,000 per month, with the listed winter regional allowance increasing it to JPY 148,000 per month from November through March. MEXT ordinarily includes qualifying tuition/fee waivers and travel support under its applicable rules.

Links:

- [Information Science and Technology pre-application procedure](https://www.ist.hokudai.ac.jp/eng/examinfo/)
- [Hokkaido University scholarships for prospective students](https://www.global.hokudai.ac.jp/admissions/scholarships/)
- [Official Study in Japan MEXT overview](https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/planning/scholarships/mext-scholarships/)

**Recommended action:** Ask Professor Sakamoto whether the October 2027 MEXT University Recommendation route would be more suitable if April 2027 and EXEX are not feasible.

## Funding routes that should not be relied upon

- **Hokkaido University Special Grant Program for Self-Supported International Students:** The Graduate School of Information Science and Technology is not currently among the participating graduate schools listed on the university scholarship page.
- **Next-Generation AI Doctoral Fellowship:** Hokkaido University states that applications ended with the April/October 2026 cohorts and that no further calls will be made. [Official notice](https://sites.google.com/elms.hokudai.ac.jp/next-gen-ai-doctoral-fellow/)
- **MEXT special-quota graduate programs in unrelated schools:** Programs for life science, environmental science, veterinary medicine, agriculture, or the Graduate School of Engineering are not routes into the HCI Lab.
- **Direct HCI Lab funding:** The lab's admission page says that the lab does not itself provide guaranteed financial support. Applicants must be self-funded or secure a scholarship independently. The lab recommends MEXT. [HCI Lab admission page](https://hci-lab.jp/en/admission.php)

## Application strategy

1. Prepare a focused two- to three-page research proposal with figures.
2. Email Professor Sakamoto at the lab address before the EXEX guidelines are released.
3. Ask for both supervision and approval/recommendation for the April 2027 EXEX application.
4. Ask whether October 2027 MEXT University Recommendation would be a better alternative.
5. Attach an academic CV, transcripts, publication list, and preliminary proposal.
6. Include a concise post-PhD career plan. The HCI Lab explicitly asks applicants to explain their career plans.
7. Treat fellowship selection and doctoral admission as separate applications.

The lab asks prospective doctoral applicants to send a strong research proposal before submitting university application documents. It recommends a PDF with figures and expects applicants to understand relevant work from venues such as ACM CHI, UIST, HRI/HAI, MobileHCI, and UbiComp.
